🔍 Keşif Soruları — Partnerinle Sor & Cevapla

  1. He / She / It öznelerinde fiile ne ekleniyor? (attend → ? , study → ?)
  2. Frequency adverb cümlede nerede duruyor? "Be" fiiliyle kullanılınca yer değişiyor mu?
  3. Beğeni fiillerinden sonra "-ing" mi "to + fiil" mi geliyor?
  4. Tercih karşılaştırırken hangi yapı kullanılıyor? (prefer X ___ Y)

Bağlamsal Okuma Parçası

B1 seviyesi · Hedef kelimeler ve dilbilgisi yapıları doğal bağlamda.

A Day in a Future-Tech High School

Nova High is not an ordinary school. Its curriculum combines traditional subjects with technology, and every student has a tablet instead of a heavy backpack. The school day starts at 9:00 a.m., so my morning routine is quite relaxed. I usually wake up at 7:30, have breakfast, and review my notes before I leave home.

According to our timetable, Mondays are the busiest. We have maths, coding, and a double science lesson. Our science teacher gives us a new assignment every two weeks, and the deadline is always on Friday. I participate in class discussions actively because I am interested in robotics.

After lunch, students can choose extracurricular activities. I attend the Robotics Club on Tuesdays and Thursdays. My friend Elif prefers the Debate Club, but my preference is always technology. She doesn't enjoy building machines, and I can't stand speaking in front of large audiences.

I look forward to Fridays most because we have a "Genius Hour" — a lesson where we work on our own projects. In short, school life at Nova High is challenging, but it is never boring.

~190 kelime · B1

Kavrama Soruları MEB Açık Uçlu Formatı

1Why is the student's morning routine described as "relaxed"? Explain with a detail from the text.
Model: Because the school day starts at 9:00 a.m., so the student can wake up at 7:30 and have a calm breakfast without rushing.
2How does the writer's preference differ from Elif's? Give reasons from the passage.
Model: The writer prefers technology and attends the Robotics Club, while Elif prefers the Debate Club. The writer can't stand speaking in front of audiences, and Elif doesn't enjoy building machines.
3What does the writer look forward to most, and why? Support your answer with evidence from the text.
Model: The writer looks forward to Fridays because of the "Genius Hour" lesson where students work on their own projects, which shows their interest in independent learning.

Mini Değerlendirme

Açık uçlu senaryolar · Speaking + Vocabulary entegre

Speaking Focus

Senaryo 1 — Club Advice: Arkadaşın Robotics mi Book Club mı seçemiyor. Çiftler halinde en az 2 hedef kelime kullanarak tavsiye verin ve gerekçenizi açıklayın.

Model Answer "You should think about what you are interested in. If you enjoy building machines, you should attend the Robotics Club, but if you prefer reading, your preference should be the Book Club. You can participate in both if your timetable allows it!"
Vocabulary Focus

Senaryo 2 — Routine Rescue: Kuzenin deadline'ları unutuyor ve hiç tekrar yapmıyor. En az 3 hedef kelimeyle bir routine önerisi yaz.

Model Answer "You should create a weekly routine. Write every assignment in a notebook and check the deadline every evening. You should revise for 30 minutes after dinner. If you never forget your tasks, you will look forward to school more."
Integrated Focus

Senaryo 3 — Ideal Timetable: İdeal timetable'ını anlat, preference'larınla ilişkilendir. En az 3 hedef kelime kullan, sonra partnerine 60 saniyede anlat.

Model Answer "In my ideal timetable, lessons start at 10 a.m. I am really interested in music, so I would attend a music club twice a week. My preference is having long breaks between lessons. I usually look forward to Fridays because we would have no assignments on weekends."
